# Agent icons

These SVGs are vendored from [Lobe Icons](https://github.com/lobehub/lobe-icons), package `@lobehub/icons-static-svg` version `1.95.0`.

Source pattern: `https://unpkg.com/@lobehub/icons-static-svg@1.95.0/icons/<name>.svg`

The local `codex.svg`, `claude.svg`, `kimi.svg`, and `gemini.svg` use the upstream `codex-color.svg`, `claude-color.svg`, `kimi-color.svg`, and `gemini-color.svg` assets. Cursor and OpenCode retain their original monochrome brand marks (`cursor.svg` and `opencode.svg`).

The upstream MIT license is included in `LICENSE`. Brand names and logos belong to their respective owners. These marks identify examples of command-capable agents; they do not represent sponsorship or a native integration.

Kimi’s white foreground path is adapted to `#20251f` for the light page background; its original blue accent (`#1783FF`) is retained.
