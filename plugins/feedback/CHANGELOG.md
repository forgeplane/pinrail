# Changelog

This plugin is released with the Pinrail app, which carries it among its
official plugins. Each version names the release of the app that first
carries it.

## [1.2.0] - Pinrail 0.4.1

### Changed

- A saved comment has edit and delete buttons at the right of its header,
  shown while the pointer is over it or the focus is in it. A click on the
  comment's text still edits it.
- A free-text answer and a comment on a question are drawn as the comment
  field of the other official plugins, with the microphone on a row under
  the text rather than in its corner.
- A comment on a question starts as one line and grows as it is written.
  Enter or its arrow saves it, and Shift+Enter starts a new line.

## [1.1.0] - Pinrail 0.3.0

### Added

- A free-text answer and a question's comment can be dictated in the
  macOS app, from a microphone in the field's corner.

### Changed

- The next and previous question are N and P. J and K now move through
  the inbox.
- The view fits a narrow frame, such as the inbox's side panel. Below
  760 pixels the groups form a strip above the questions, and below 520
  pixels the counts sit under the title. Where the questions are
  narrower than 700 pixels, a question's buttons and its requirement sit
  under its prompt, so a long prompt keeps the width of the card.

## [1.0.0] - Pinrail 0.1.0

The first version. It shows questions an agent needs answered before it
goes on, grouped and conditional, with an optional recommended answer
and a comment on any answer.
