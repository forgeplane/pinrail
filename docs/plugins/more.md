Besides the plugins that come with the app, more official plugins are developed in [forgeplane/pinrail-plugins](https://github.com/forgeplane/pinrail-plugins). Each one is installed from its folder or zip, as described in [Installing plugins](/docs/using/installing-plugins/).

| Plugin | What it is for |
|---|---|
| Email | Drafts an agent wants to send, edited with the changes showing, then sent, revised or discarded one by one. |
| Artifact | An HTML page an agent designed, commented on element by element, with selectors sent back to the agent. |
| Calendar | Times to arrange around what is already booked: one suggested slot picked per item, another time asked for, or the item declined. |
| Logo | Candidate logo marks seen where they will live, a favourite picked, and changes asked for on parts of a mark. |
| 3D model | Candidate 3D models on a stage to orbit, a favourite picked, and changes asked for on parts of a model. |
| Design canvas | A set of designs on a canvas, such as the steps of a flow or variants of a screen, commented on and approved frame by frame. |
| Audio | Audio takes, such as voices reading one script, compared with their waveforms and transcripts, commented at a moment, and cut. |
| Video | A video, such as a promo cut or a screen recording, commented at a moment or over a stretch, on its picture or its sound. |
| Motion | Candidate animations, in CSS or Lottie, played frame by frame and side by side, with a favourite picked. |
| Palette | Colour palettes or design tokens, painted on a sample screen in light and dark, with their contrast measured. |
| Before and after | The images of a revision, compared with a wipe, side by side, a fade or a difference highlight, each request marked fixed or not. |
| Trade | A trade an agent proposes, on its candlestick chart with the entry, stop and targets to adjust, approved or rejected. |

Each plugin's README in that repository describes what its agent sends and what comes back.
